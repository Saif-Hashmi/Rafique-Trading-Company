About Us

Our Story

15+ years of importing and manufacturing garments in Lahore, Pakistan

Who We Are

Built on Trust, Backed by Experience

Rafique Trading Company (RTC) is a Lahore-based importer and manufacturer of garments, registered with the Chamber of Commerce and an active NTN holder of over 15 years. What began as a small trading operation has grown into a full manufacturing and wholesale business serving retailers and buyers across the region.

Our business covers the full cycle: importing quality garments, manufacturing local garments — including jeans — from raw material to finished goods, and wholesaling everything directly from our outlet in New Anarkali, Lahore.

How We Work

From Raw Material to Wholesale Shelf

1

Source & Import

We import quality garments and source raw materials for local manufacturing.

2

Manufacture Locally

Our team manufactures local garments, including jeans, turning raw material into finished goods in-house.

3

Wholesale at Anarkali

Finished goods move to our New Anarkali outlet, where they're wholesaled to retailers and buyers.
