About Us

A Trusted Name in Lahore's Garment Trade

For over 15 years, Rafique Trading Company (RTC) has built a reputation as a reliable importer and manufacturer of garments. We manufacture local garments, including jeans, taking them from cutting and stitching through to finished goods, and we import quality garments to complement our range.

All finished goods are wholesaled directly to retailers and buyers from our outlet in New Anarkali, Lahore — giving our clients competitive pricing and consistent quality.
