Our Process

From Fabric to Finished Goods

Local manufacturing at RTC follows a controlled process: raw material and fabric are sourced, garments (including jeans) are cut and stitched in-house, finished through washing and quality checks, then moved to our New Anarkali outlet for wholesale.

This end-to-end control lets us maintain consistent quality and offer competitive wholesale pricing to our retail and business partners.
